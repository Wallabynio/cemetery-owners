Presentation site for **UTS 17912 Development Negotiation and Community Engagement, Assessment 2 Part A**. We're negotiating as the **Cemetery Owners** in Scenario 1: the Lane Cove National Park access road, North Ryde.

## What this is

This site is what we'll present from in Week 5, once the negotiation (Wednesday 29 July 2026) is done and we have a real outcome to show. Right now, ahead of that, we're building it: our prep, our proposal, and a place to log the live negotiation as it happens.

## Viewing it locally

No build step — it's plain HTML/CSS/JS. Either:

- Double-click `index.html` to open it in a browser, or
- From this folder, run a local server so relative links behave exactly like the deployed site:
  ```
  python3 -m http.server 8000
  ```
  then open `http://localhost:8000`.

The two documents behind the case are quoted in full on `index.html` (collapsible sections, no download needed). Their plain-text source lives in `assets/docs/`:

The map on the homepage shows the real-world location the brief describes: Delhi Road, North Ryde, becoming Lady Game Drive at the Lane Cove National Park entrance, with Northern Suburbs Memorial Gardens and Crematorium (a privately operated cemetery) sitting on that road between the station and the park — a reasonable real-world match for the brief, not a claim made by the assignment itself.
