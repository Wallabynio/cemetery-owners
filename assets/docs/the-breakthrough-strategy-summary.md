# The Breakthrough Strategy

1. Don't react — **GO TO THE BALCONY**
2. Don't argue — **STEP TO THEIR SIDE**
3. Don't reject — **REFRAME**
4. Don't push — **BUILD THEM A GOLDEN BRIDGE**
5. Don't escalate — **USE POWER TO EDUCATE**

*Turn adversaries into partners.*

## 1. Don't react — Go to the balcony

Be aware of three natural reactions:
- Striking back
- Giving in
- Breaking off

Be aware of the dangers of reacting — **go to the balcony**.

Recognise their tactics. Name the game they are playing. Three kinds of tactics are commonly used:
- Stone walls
- Attacks
- Tricks

Know your own hot buttons:
- Don't get angry
- Don't get even
- Get what you want

Buy time to think:
- Pause and say nothing
- Rewind the tape
- Take a time out
- Don't make important decisions on the spot

## 2. Don't argue — Step to their side

Listen actively:
- Hear the other side's point of view
- Paraphrase and ask for corrections

Acknowledge their point of view:
- Acknowledge their feelings
- Offer an apology
- Project confidence

Agree whenever you can:
- Agree without conceding
- Accumulated yesses!
- Tune in to their wavelength

Acknowledge the person:
- Acknowledge their authority and competence — show respect
- Build a working relationship

Express your views without provoking:
- Replace 'but' with 'and'
- Make "I" statements rather than 'you' statements
- Stand up for yourself
- Acknowledge your differences with optimism

Create a favourable climate for negotiation.

## 3. Don't reject — Reframe

To change the game, change the frame.

Ask problem-solving questions:
- Find out "why" or "why not" but avoid using the word "why" — instead use "what", "how" and "when" to start your questions
- Ask for their advice
- Ask "What would make that fair for both?"
- Questions should be open-ended
- Tap the power of silence

Reframe tactics:
- **Go around stone walls** — ignore the stone wall; reinterpret it as an aspiration; take it seriously, but test it
- **Deflect attacks** — ignore the attack; reframe an attack on you as an attack on the problem; reframe a personal attack as friendly; reframe from past wrongs to future remedies; reframe from "you and me" to "we"
- **Expose tricks** — ask clarifying questions; make a reasonable request; turn the trick to your advantage

Negotiate about the rules of the game:
- Bring it up
- Negotiate about the negotiation

Identify the turning point.

## 4. Don't push — Build a golden bridge

Obstacles to agreement:
- Not their idea
- Unmet interests
- Fear of losing face
- Too much too fast

Build a golden bridge. Involve the other side:
- Ask for and build on their ideas
- Ask for constructive criticism
- Offer them a choice

Satisfy unmet interests:
- Don't dismiss them as irrational
- Don't overlook basic human needs
- Don't assume a fixed pie
- Look for low-cost high-benefit trades
- Use an if-then formula

Help them save face:
- Help them back away without backing down
- Show how circumstances have changed
- Ask for a third-party recommendation
- Point to a standard of fairness
- Help write their victory speech

Go slow to go fast:
- Guide them step by step
- Don't ask for a final commitment until the end
- Don't rush to the finish

## 5. Don't escalate — Use power to educate

Let them know the consequences. Ask reality-testing questions:
- "What do you think will happen if we don't agree?"
- "What do you think I will do?"
- "What will you do?"

Warn but don't threaten — demonstrate your BATNA.

Use your BATNA to defuse their reaction:
- Deploy your BATNA without provoking
- Use the minimum power necessary
- Use legitimate means

Neutralise their attacks. Tap the third force:
- Build a coalition
- Use third parties to stop attacks
- Use third parties to promote negotiations

Keep sharpening their choice:
- Let them know they have a way out — don't back them into a corner
- Let them choose
- Even when you can win, negotiate!

Forge a lasting agreement:
- Keep implementation in mind
- Minimise your risks
- Build a dispute resolution procedure
- Reaffirm the relationship

Ask for minimal satisfaction — not victory!

**TURN ADVERSARIES INTO PARTNERS**
