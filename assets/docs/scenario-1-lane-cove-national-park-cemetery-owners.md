# Scenario 1 - Lane Cove National Park access road, North Ryde

## Negotiation position - Cemetery owners

### Challenges

- Believes the Council should fix the stormwater pollution problem by upgrading road drainage.
- Doesn't want to bear the full cost for the stormwater works.
- Denies National Park's claim that stormwater runoff from the cemetery is causing weed and sediment problems. Believes that general runoff down the road is a major part of the pollution problem, which strengthens its case that it is the Council's responsibility to fix it up.

### Opportunities

- Knows that the Council might eventually force it to upgrade its stormwater system at its own cost and likes the idea of sharing the cost three ways.
- Could potentially establish a stormwater detention pond on site, which could provide irrigation water for Cemetery lawns, thereby reducing reticulated water costs.
