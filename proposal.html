Cemetery Owners (us)

Our proposal

Agreed as a group by 26 July and sent to Council and National Park ahead of the 29 July session — and what comes back from them.

What we sent

The letter below went to Council and National Park by 26 July, ahead of the 29 July session.

To: Council negotiation team, National Park negotiation team
From: Cemetery Owners negotiation team
Re: Scenario 1 — Lane Cove National Park access road, North Ryde

How we see the problem

We agree the road needs fixing. The bushfire-evacuation risk on the narrow section is real, and none of us wants the commuter parking or the pollution running into the Park to carry on. Arguing about who's to blame won't get us anywhere — what will is agreeing a fair way to split the $2M of work in front of us.

An idea that could offset the cost

We're open to building an on-site stormwater detention pond at the Cemetery. Done well, it deals with the stormwater and gives us a water source for irrigation, so part of the spend comes back to us as a benefit rather than a straight cost. We think that's worth reflecting in how the stormwater cost gets split.

Our opening position

Cost itemAmountOur proposed share
Road widening / resurfacing / footpath$1.2MNot a Cemetery cost — doesn't benefit our operations
Parking meters$0.3MNot a Cemetery cost — commuter-driven, not cemetery-driven
Stormwater improvements$0.5MWe'll contribute a share, in exchange for a detention-pond design we can also use for irrigation

Before we lock in any split on the stormwater, we'd like the three of us to jointly commission an independent hydrology assessment, so we're all working from the same evidence instead of trading assumptions.

What we're asking of Council and National Park

  • Council: confirm what state/RMS funding exists for the road and parking before we treat the full $2M as ours alone to cover.
  • National Park: share the monitoring data behind the stormwater claim, so the hydrology work has a starting point.
  • Both: agree to the detention-pond concept in principle, subject to a design that also serves Cemetery irrigation.

Process

On 29 July we'd like to settle the cost-sharing principle and the detention-pond concept, and leave detailed design and timing to a short follow-up. We're ready to move off this position — it's a starting point, not a final offer.

Our fallback range

Full detail on the Preparation page. In short: our ceiling was ~50% of the stormwater cost, conditional on the detention pond — and we opened well below it.
